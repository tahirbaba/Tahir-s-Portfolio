Sawal

Owners aksar ye poochhte hain

Mera purana WhatsApp number chalega?

Zyada tar haan. Setup ke waqt check kiya jata hai ke aap ka number WhatsApp Business App ke saath chalta rahe. Na ho sake to order ke liye alag number liya ja sakta hai.

Kya AI ghalat price bata dega?

Nahi. Prices, delivery charges aur total aap ke menu se system calculate karta hai; AI sirf baat karta hai. Menu badlein to agli chat se nayi price lagti hai.

Mere apne riders hain, wo kaise kaam karenge?

Wahi riders. Un ke mobile pe ek page khulta hai: kaunsa order uthana hai, address, map aur kitna cash lena hai.

Customer ghussa ho ya shikayat kare to?

AI chat aap ke staff ko de deta hai. Aap ka banda apni WhatsApp Business App se jawab deta hai, aur kaam khatam hone pe chat wapas AI ko.

WhatsApp ka kharcha kaun deta hai?

Meta ka bill seedha aap ke WhatsApp account pe aata hai: har number ke 1,000 messages mahana free, phir ~PKR 4 per message. System is tarah bana hai ke har order pe kam se kam messages jaayein.

Pehle aazma sakte hain?

Haan, 14 din free trial. Trial mein har hafte report milti hai: kitne orders aaye, kitni sale hui, raat ke kitne orders the.
